Web3 / Security

Kaizen

The Problem

On-chain activity can hide abnormal or malicious behaviour that goes unnoticed until damage is already done, and most teams only find out after funds or trust are lost.

The Idea

Build a system that watches on-chain activity continuously, learns what normal behaviour looks like, and raises an alert the moment something deviates from it.

How It Works

Smart contract activity is streamed into a monitoring layer, which is analysed for anomalies. Flagged activity is scored and surfaced as a security alert before it can cause further harm.

My Contribution

Worked with teammates Divyam Singh and Mayur Shelke across ideation, development, debugging, rapid iteration, and the final judge presentation during the 48-hour build window.

Tech Stack

Next.jsNode.jsFastAPISolidityEthereumMachine Learning

The Outcome

A functional Web3 security prototype, built and demoed within 48 hours at ETHMumbai.

What I Learned

How to move fast without cutting corners on the core idea, and how to explain a technical system clearly to judges who are seeing it for the first time.

AI × Blockchain

Aegis

The Problem

Digital interactions need both intelligent threat detection and a way to prove that a record hasn't been tampered with after the fact.

The Idea

Pair AI-based threat detection with blockchain-backed verification, so flagged events are not only caught but also permanently and transparently recorded.

How It Works

Incoming activity is analysed by an AI model for threat signals, scored for risk, and — where relevant — anchored to a blockchain record for tamper-evident verification.

My Contribution

Designed the system architecture covering threat analysis, verification, and tamper-evident record-keeping, and helped integrate the AI and blockchain layers into one workflow.

Tech Stack

Next.jsFastAPIMongoDBAIBlockchain

The Outcome

A working web platform demonstrating an end-to-end flow from threat detection to blockchain-backed verification.

What I Learned

How to design a system where two different technologies (AI and blockchain) genuinely need each other, instead of bolting one onto the other for the sake of it.

Live Demo GitHub
AI × DAO × UX

Omnix

The Problem

Decentralized governance is powerful but hard to use: proposals, voting power, and treasury state are scattered and cognitively heavy for everyday members to follow.

The Idea

Turn complex governance mechanics into an intent-driven flow — proposing, voting, and reviewing decisions — with AI assistance guiding members through it.

How It Works

A proposal moves through contextual AI assistance that summarises intent and impact, then into community voting, with voting power, proposals, and treasury state visualised throughout.

My Contribution

Designed the AI-powered DAO platform end to end, translating blockchain governance mechanics into intuitive, user-centred workflows and visual states.

Tech Stack

Web3DAO GovernanceAIUX

The Outcome

A governance interface concept that reduces the cognitive load of participating in a DAO, from reading a proposal to casting an informed vote.

What I Learned

That good UX for Web3 isn't about hiding the blockchain — it's about translating what's actually happening on-chain into language and visuals a person can act on.
